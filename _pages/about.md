I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at the [University of Notre Dame](https://www.nd.edu/), where I work as a Graduate Research Assistant in the [Data Security and Privacy Lab](https://sites.nd.edu/taeho-jung/dsp-lab/) advised by [Prof. Taeho Jung](https://sites.nd.edu/taeho-jung/).

My research focuses on **distributed systems, security, and confidential computing**. I build systems that let mutually distrusting parties share and compute on data with strong integrity and confidentiality guarantees — including decentralized multi-tenant databases ([MtDB](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11264649/), IEEE Blockchain 2025) and secure logging protocols for decentralized data sharing ([SecLog](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-06323-6_9), METAVERSE 2025).

Before my PhD, I spent about 3.5 years as a software engineer at multinational software companies in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I received my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from the [University of Dhaka](https://www.du.ac.bd/) in 2020, where my thesis on blockchain-based log management was supervised by [Dr. Mosarrat Jahan](https://www.du.ac.bd/faculty/faculty_details/CSE/1777) and [Dr. Upama Kabir](https://du.ac.bd/index.php/faculty/faculty_details/CSE/1765).
